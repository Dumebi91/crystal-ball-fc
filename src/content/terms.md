### 4. The games and how we score them

All times are West Africa Time. Full rules for each game are on the [How it works](/how) page. The scoring is:

| Game | When | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know Ball | Opens 9am, closes midnight, one go a day | 1 point per right answer, plus 5 for all 10 right. The 60-second clock runs only while a question is on screen. |
| Call It | Each game locks at its kick-off | 10 points for the exact score, 3 for the right result |
| Call It International | Each game locks at its kick-off | Same as Call It |
| Poll of the Day | Opens 12pm | 1 point for voting |
| The Ten (coming later this season) | 6pm on its days | 1 point per name, plus 2 for the full list. It counts on the tables but never decides a prize. |
| Cold Takes | 12pm and 6pm | No points |

- **Results:** we use the official score at the end of normal time, including stoppage time, not extra time or penalties.
- **Postponed or abandoned games:** calls on them score nothing.
- **Oracle of the Week:** the most points in a matchweek, crowned at noon the day after the matchweek's last game. Ties go to the most exact scores, then the most right Know Ball answers, then the fastest total Know Ball time.
- **Ranks:** Reader at 150 season points, Seer at 500, Oracle at 1,200. Prophet is the top 1% at the end of the season.
- **Mistakes:** if a question turns out to be wrong or unclear, or a score is entered wrongly, we'll fix it, recalculate points and tell members on the site. If you think your score is wrong, tell us within 7 days at support@crystalballfc.com.
