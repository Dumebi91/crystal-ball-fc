Crystal Ball FC is a free football fan community at crystalballfc.com: a daily quiz, score predictions for points, polls, takes and leagues. {{OPERATOR}} This policy explains what we collect about you, why, who sees it and what you can do about it. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the rules of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

The short version: we collect what we need to run the games, we never sell your data, your phone number is never shown to anyone, and you can download or delete your data at any time.

### 1. What we collect

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account details | Phone number, handle, city, the club you support (optional), your confirmation that you're 18 or over | You, when you join |
| Game activity | Quiz answers and how long each took, score predictions, poll votes, points, ranks, streaks, badges, leagues you create or join | Your play on the site |
| What you post | Votes on Cold Takes, replies in the Dressing Room, reports you make | You |
| Device and usage | IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, approximate location from your IP, error logs | Your device, automatically |
| Fair-play signals | Answer timings, question order, how answers match other accounts, login patterns | Your play, automatically |
| Prize details (winners only) | Full name, ID document for age and identity checks, delivery address, phone number for data bundles | You, if you win |
| Your settings | Reminder choices | You |

We don't collect sensitive data such as your religion, health or political views, and we ask you not to post it in replies.

### 2. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis under the Act |

| Run leagues, Cold Takes and the Dressing Room | Contract |

### 3. What other members can see

Your handle, city, club, rank, points, badges, streak, league memberships and replies are public on the site. Leaderboards, leagues and the Oracle of the Week crowning show them. We may also feature a great call or reply in our posts and videos, credited to your handle (see the [Terms](/terms)). Your phone number, ID and address are never shown.

### 4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data, and we never give it to betting companies.

We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, under contracts that limit what they can do with it:

- **Hosting and database:** Vercel and Supabase
- **Login codes by text message:** Termii
- **Football fixtures and results:** API-Football (this receives no personal data)
- **Videos:** YouTube and TikTok. Their players only load when you press play, and from then on their own privacy policies apply.
- **Prize delivery, winners only:** the courier or network that delivers your prize

Sponsors and partners only ever get totals, such as "62% of members picked Arsenal". They never get your name, handle or contact details unless you agree to it for a specific prize or offer.

We will share data with the police, a regulator or a court only when the law requires it.

### 5. Where your data is stored

Some of our providers store data on servers outside Nigeria, mainly in the European Union (our database is in Frankfurt) and the United States. Before we transfer it, we make sure it is protected to the standard the Act requires, through the provider's data protection terms and contract clauses, as section 42 of the Act allows. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

### 7. Your rights

Under the Act you can:

- see the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- correct anything that's wrong;
- ask us to delete your data;
- ask us to limit how we use it, or object to a use based on our legitimate interest;
- take your data to another service in a common electronic format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before;
- ask for a person to review any decision made about you by an automated system.

You can download your data and delete your account yourself on the Me page. For anything else, email dee@crystalballfc.com. We'll reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm it's you first.

### 8. Fair-play checks

Our systems flag unusual patterns, such as answers that match another account too closely or answers faster than a person could read the question. A flag on its own never costs you anything. A member of our team reviews every flag before we remove points, withhold a prize or suspend an account, and you can ask us to look again.

### 9. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in. These are always on, because the site doesn't work without them. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we'll only set those cookies if you click Accept on a cookie banner, and we'll update this policy first.

### 10. Age

Crystal Ball FC is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we find that a member is under 18, we'll close the account and delete their data. If you think a child has joined, tell us at dee@crystalballfc.com.

### 11. Security and breaches

We use encryption in transit, access controls and one-time login codes, and we limit staff access to people who need it. No system is completely secure, so if a breach puts your data at risk we'll tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of finding out. Where the risk to you is high, we'll tell you directly and quickly, with what happened and what you should do.
